EnviroGuard simulator

An interactive model of the PRD’s behaviour: thresholds, hysteresis, sensor-fault detection and the mute button. Use it to learn what the real board should do before you flash it.

Rehearsal tool only. The hackathon requires firmware running on a physical ESP32. A simulation can’t be submitted as evidence or as your project.

Sensor and controls

  • Raise temperature past 30 °C: red LED, state ALARM (REQ-04).
  • Drop it to 29.5 °C: stays in alarm until below 29 °C (hysteresis, REQ-05).
  • Unplug the sensor: after 3 failed reads the red LED blinks at 5 Hz (REQ-08).
  • Press the button in ALARM: LED off, state still ALARM (REQ-09).
